# Known limitations

This branch is not a working TWLTL traffic mod. It must not be published as one.

- The Road Builder adapter and diagnostics compile and pass official postprocessing against installed CS2 1.6.4f1 and Road Builder 0.8.0.0. A fresh-prefab fix passed live closed-section catalogue discovery, picker search and placement in a clean two-mod playset. The resulting road config has the section between four car-lane groups, and the road widened by 3 m. Placed-owner native capture and same-process local save/load passed for an isolated closed road; restart, moving cars and removal safety remain untested. See LOCAL_VERIFICATION.md for exact binary identities.
- The installed `Invisible Car Twoway Lane 3` has a direct section/piece source chain. The default-off native fixture registered and appeared in Road Builder's picker; its preconfigured road was placed disconnected in a disposable city. Selected-owner captures found two consecutive native edge `CarLaneFlags.Twoway` lanes with an exact shared path node and zero endpoint gap. A pathfinder lookup found no direct edge for either center lane or two adjacent ordinary lanes. Later captures identified the sampled center and adjacent lanes as `SlaveLane`s; the installed pathfinding job explicitly skips slave lanes. The center and adjacent slaves shared group 1 and `masterIndex=0`; their `MasterLane` used the center prefab but had no `Twoway` car flag and had a forward-only graph edge. Installed `PathUtils.GetCarDriveSpecification` requires a graph-owning `Twoway` flag to add a backward edge. A second disconnected fixture set `NetSectionInfo.m_Median` for the native center section; its live capture at `18:15:59` produced the same center/ordinary slave group and forward-only master edge. Thus neither isolated fixture establishes a bidirectional route or qualifies for a car trial. Opposing braking and turn-only routing remain untested. The CLOSED research section remains non-drivable.
- A third opt-in separator fixture initially failed registration because `Road Median 0` contains no qualifying zero-width `BlockTraffic` piece in this installation. The revised fixture creates its own lane-free virtual piece. It registered and was placed disconnected; the `18:34:36` capture found a direct center `Twoway` lane with `Forward` and `Backward` path-edge flags, with no master/slave grouping. The flag may block lateral entry into the center lane. Physical access, turn-only routing and opposing-vehicle safeguards remain unproven, so it is not a functional lane or suitable for connected traffic.
- A fourth default-off fixture inserts a zero-width non-road lane instead of a traffic blocker. This is only a graph-grouping hypothesis. It compiled and passed official postprocessing against installed assemblies; it has not been registered or placed in a live city. It has no traffic safeguards.
- The default-off `TWTL NATIVE TWO-WAY UNSAFE TEST 3m` fixture is for disconnected disposable roads and native graph inspection only. It has no restriction against through routing or conflicting car entry; connecting it to traffic is outside the current test gate.
- A second default-off `TWTL MEDIAN-FLAG GRAPH TEST 3m` fixture marks the same invisible native car section as a median in Road Builder's generated network metadata. It is an untested graph-grouping experiment, not physical-compatibility validation or a functional lane. Its helper road is likewise restricted to disconnected disposable placement; no car test is authorized by its registration alone.
- The opt-in section has no car lanes. It occupies plain surface and provides no actual turn markings or TWLTL traffic function. It is explicitly labelled CLOSED. Once registered, turning the setting off does not unregister a live prefab; restart removes the picker entry.
- There is no verified representation of opposite logical lanes, native `LaneOverlap` injection, graph restriction, entry enforcement, car braking/speed integration, native reservation adapter or lane-change actuator. All game traffic activation is unavailable.
- The installed native overlap generator skips ordinary edge-lane pairs. Automatic full-length reciprocal overlaps must not be assumed. Static inspection does not establish whether manually supplied opposing overlaps brake safely; that still requires the isolated native experiment.
- Reverse-curve diagnostics compare all four cubic control points within 5 cm and reject ambiguous endpoints. They deliberately miss differently parameterized equivalent curves, partial overlaps and closed loops. Candidate detection is geometry evidence only, never permission to activate traffic.
- Configuration validation accepts explicit physical strip classifications. It is not connected to an automatic pre-generation Road Builder validator or a placed-network classifier. Runtime evidence is caller-supplied; it is not inferred from member presence.
- Core calculations support arbitrary compatible lane counts, asymmetry and both handedness conventions, but this has only been tested in the standalone model.
- Full route swept intervals are conservative, including for same-direction vehicles. Space is not reused merely because an ETA passed. Fairness/priority ageing and native lane-object occupancy reconstruction are not implemented.
- Overdue occupied vehicles retain safety holds and close admissions. Automatic stuck-vehicle recovery needs real occupancy, a verified exit and native navigation integration. Releasing a hold by timeout would be unsafe.
- Gap advice assumes complete local observations and constant-speed neighbours. It considers three acceleration choices; it is not an exhaustive search and may yield despite a feasible gap. No result authorizes a lane change. Hysteresis currently affects preferences among candidate gaps and is tested for unchanged-observation stability, not fluctuating game traffic.
- Intersection transition intervals are calculated; actual lane geometry, turn connection generation and signals are not changed. Short segments get a closed fallback.
- Tram support is not runtime-tested. Crossable tracks produce a configuration WARNING; impassable tracks are INVALID. Native turning-car/tram conflict processing is unknown.
- One local save/load in the same process preserved the isolated closed road's Road Builder cross-section and inspectable lane count. Game restart, updates, mod disposal and removal have not been tested with research roads. Road Builder documents rebuilding roads without missing custom sections. This can change a road layout; do not assume uninstall safety or general save compatibility. A startup warning said the custom section could not be found before later picker/edit success; its lifecycle implications remain open.
- No automated compatibility enforcement for other mods exists. The API audit logs assemblies loaded at that instant; it can miss later-loaded assemblies. Source review identified overlapping systems but no combined playset was tested.
- The current managed controller allocates during batches and reconciliation, is not thread-safe or Burst-ready, and has no measured large-city budget. Diagnostic captures may synchronize jobs and stall; they run only when requested and disclose truncated counts.
- Only actual research controls appear in game options. Intended/exit/urgent distances, speed, safety clearance and transition length are configurable constructor parameters in the tested core; player-facing traffic settings and debug visualization are intentionally not exposed until they have a working game adapter.
- No Paradox dependency metadata/package or release is provided. Road Builder is the intended required mod; the others are optional research/compatibility targets.
