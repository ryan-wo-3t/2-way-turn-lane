# Known limitations

- The 1.6.4f1 route trace now distinguishes visible center-to-junction and center-to-ordinary-lane paths from possible roadside turns, with a 50 m center-distance check. It is an observation used by the two-minute traffic monitor, not vehicle admission. A `PossibleRoadsideTurn` label does not prove a legal destination, physical crossing, available gap, or reservation. Installed `PathUtils.GetCarDriveSpecification` gives `Forbidden` a cost while leaving the center edge traversable; `PathfindJobs` applies that cost to a shared graph edge without knowing a particular car's final turn. The current game adapter therefore cannot enforce per-car turn-only access through the existing flag. Do not enable the prototype outside the disposable test city.
- Experimental Road Builder center sections now have a deployed native full-lane blockage gate. It compiles against installed 1.6.4f1. A Bonham capture confirmed that one native two-way lane had full blockage in its component and both primary and secondary path graph edges, with zero mismatches. A synthetic route probe returned no path in either direction, while two open-lane controls using the same query setup returned positive paths. This supports hard route denial on the isolated closed fixture. It is a complete closure, not turn-only behavior. Existing-car behavior has not been live-verified. Vehicles already occupying a lane may retain old navigation until the game replans. The gate only recognizes Road Builder configurations containing this add-on's experimental section names and the installed `Invisible Car Twoway Lane 3` prefab. Disabling the mod or an unresolved/generated configuration can leave the experimental road unprotected. Do not use these sections as a functional shared turn lane.

This branch is not a working TWLTL traffic mod. It must not be published as one.

- The connected 27 m V3 prototype on Bonham's Honeysuckle Lane carried real traffic in a two-minute CS2 1.6.4f1 test on 2026-11-23. A once-per-second `CarCurrentLane` sample counted 23 distinct cars on its ordinary lanes and six distinct cars on its native two-way center lane. The center-entry routes led toward roundabout node lanes, often with `TurnRight`, and did not prove legitimate roadside left turns. Actual center occupancy is now established, but turn-only use, opposing-car braking, conflict control and safe junction transitions are not. The opt-in V3 road is unsafe and limited to the disposable test city.
- The 2026-11-25 V3 junction gate closed 15 native node lanes in Bonham, yet a first two-minute monitor observed eight distinct cars on the center, including six near-full-length trips toward a roundabout. A later two-minute monitor from a post-gate autosave saw 35 distinct ordinary-lane cars and **zero center-lane cars**. Old routes clearing is one plausible explanation; the test does not prove new-route denial or a usable left-turn route. Lateral entry and exit may still permit through travel, while valid roadside turns may be inaccessible. There is no enforced per-vehicle intent or conflict reservation. Do not treat this gate as a functional turn lane.
- The gate closes the V3 center path itself if either junction endpoint has no exact connector match. This is a conservative fallback, not a proof that matched connectors make the center safe. It may leave a previously closed center closed after an option toggle; reopening without verified routing would be unsafe.
- A matched V3 center gains native `CarLaneFlags.Forbidden`, which adds a pathfinding cost in installed 1.6.4f1. The edge remains available and may still be chosen for a through trip. The Bonham monitor observed center travel after this flag was applied; it did not establish when those cars' routes were calculated. This cost is not an authorization rule.
- The 2026-11-25 one-shot diagnostic initially sampled only the first 64 of 133 V3 sub-lanes and missed the center. The corrected capture was live-verified on the same road: it included the late center, found 15 exact matching junction links with full graph blockage on logged primary edges, and found an open bidirectional center graph path of 86.8934 m. Full junction blockage does not by itself enforce turn-only travel through the center.

- Bonham (`TWTL Traffic Test`) supplies an ordinary-road traffic baseline. A direct `CarCurrentLane` query at `17:59:26` found two selected-road active cars, each with 12 near-term navigation segments and remaining path data. The Road Builder editor showed four ordinary car lanes and no experimental TWTL section on the two inspected Lake Street pieces. A later capture selected a stock-derived `Medium Road` with zero experimental sections and three active cars; that owner cannot be conclusively matched to the inspected pieces. No left turn into a shared lane was tested, and the road layout was unchanged.
- The first route diagnostic described 32 upcoming path targets, but its five sampled path owners had no active-car components or navigation-lane buffers. A filtered lane-occupant sampler then found zero active cars. The direct car query resolved that sampling issue. Its navigation showed a native `TurnRight, Roundabout` segment, which does not prove a roadside turn. No capture proves intended shared-lane turns, entry eligibility or conflict safety; no runtime traffic gate was satisfied.

- The Road Builder adapter and diagnostics compile and pass official postprocessing against installed CS2 1.6.4f1 and Road Builder 0.8.0.0. A fresh-prefab fix passed live closed-section catalogue discovery, picker search and placement in a clean two-mod playset. The resulting road config has the section between four car-lane groups, and the road widened by 3 m. Placed-owner native capture and same-process local save/load passed for an isolated closed road; restart, moving cars and removal safety remain untested. See LOCAL_VERIFICATION.md for exact binary identities.
- The installed `Invisible Car Twoway Lane 3` has a direct section/piece source chain. The default-off native fixture registered and appeared in Road Builder's picker; its preconfigured road was placed disconnected in a disposable city. Selected-owner captures found two consecutive native edge `CarLaneFlags.Twoway` lanes with an exact shared path node and zero endpoint gap. A pathfinder lookup found no direct edge for either center lane or two adjacent ordinary lanes. Later captures identified the sampled center and adjacent lanes as `SlaveLane`s; the installed pathfinding job explicitly skips slave lanes. The center and adjacent slaves shared group 1 and `masterIndex=0`; their `MasterLane` used the center prefab but had no `Twoway` car flag and had a forward-only graph edge. Installed `PathUtils.GetCarDriveSpecification` requires a graph-owning `Twoway` flag to add a backward edge. A second disconnected fixture set `NetSectionInfo.m_Median` for the native center section; its live capture at `18:15:59` produced the same center/ordinary slave group and forward-only master edge. Thus neither isolated fixture establishes a bidirectional route or qualifies for a car trial. Opposing braking and turn-only routing remain untested. The CLOSED research section remains non-drivable.
- A third opt-in separator fixture initially failed registration because `Road Median 0` contains no qualifying zero-width `BlockTraffic` piece in this installation. The revised fixture creates its own lane-free virtual piece. It registered and was placed disconnected; the `18:34:36` capture found a direct center `Twoway` lane with `Forward` and `Backward` path-edge flags, with no master/slave grouping. The flag may block lateral entry into the center lane. Physical access, turn-only routing and opposing-vehicle safeguards remain unproven, so it is not a functional lane or suitable for connected traffic.
- A fourth default-off fixture inserts a zero-width non-road lane instead of a traffic blocker. It compiled and passed official postprocessing against installed assemblies, registered, and was placed disconnected in a disposable city. The `18:51:00` capture found three direct car lanes without master/slave grouping. The center lane had `Twoway` and a primary path edge with both `Forward` and `Backward`; the ordinary lanes had forward-only edges. All three car lanes reported carriageway group 0. The zero-width lane had no `CarLane` component. This establishes isolated graph ownership only; lateral access, turn-only routing and opposing-car safety remain unproven. The fixture has no traffic safeguards.
- The read-only endpoint-node diagnostic ran live on a disconnected virtual-lane fixture in a disposable city. The selected edge had a direct two-way center graph edge, but its generated car lane carried `ForbidPassing` and had zero `LaneOverlap` records. At a junction with another fixture edge, the game generated 13 car node lanes, including center-touching links marked `UTurnLeft`, `Unsafe`, and `Twoway`. This is evidence of native candidate connectors, not proof of turn-only routing, lateral entry, or safe opposing traffic. The fixture remains disconnected from traffic.
- A later disposable-city attempt connected the virtual-lane fixture between ordinary roads. The one-shot capture still found two direct bidirectional center graph edges, but zero lane occupants. Homes beyond the fixture lacked power, water and sewage, and the helper road has no verified utility continuity. The attempt did not demonstrate vehicle traversability or safety and must not be repeated as a car trial until the traffic controls are implemented.
- The staged virtual-lane helper now requests Road Builder's underground water, sewer and electricity sections plus zoning. This is compile-verified but not live-tested. Saved helper-road configurations keep their previous add-on flags and are only warned about; a code update does not silently rewrite a save.
- Installed 1.6.4f1 `CarLaneSpeedIterator` calls `CheckCurrentLane` for car lanes, including a native two-way lane; that method distinguishes opposite curve-position ordering, labels such occupants `Oncoming`, and passes them to `UpdateMaxSpeed`. Thus zero `LaneOverlap` records do not alone prove that native same-lane braking is absent. No opposing occupants or braking outcome have been observed in this fixture. The one-shot diagnostic now reports bounded center-lane occupant direction counts for a future isolated trial.
- The default-off `TWTL NATIVE TWO-WAY UNSAFE TEST 3m` fixture is for disconnected disposable roads and native graph inspection only. It has no restriction against through routing or conflicting car entry; connecting it to traffic is outside the current test gate.
- The one-shot selected-road capture now makes at most six bounded native path queries: both directions on up to two direct two-way car lanes, plus two across the pair when their path nodes touch. It reports whether a positive-distance path includes both requested lane targets. No live result has been recorded yet. Even a successful pair query cannot establish legal entry from an ordinary lane, exclude through traffic, or authorize a vehicle trial.
- A second default-off `TWTL MEDIAN-FLAG GRAPH TEST 3m` fixture marks the same invisible native car section as a median in Road Builder's generated network metadata. It is an untested graph-grouping experiment, not physical-compatibility validation or a functional lane. Its helper road is likewise restricted to disconnected disposable placement; no car test is authorized by its registration alone.
- The opt-in section has no car lanes. It occupies plain surface and provides no actual turn markings or TWLTL traffic function. It is explicitly labelled CLOSED. Once registered, turning the setting off does not unregister a live prefab; restart removes the picker entry.
- There is no verified representation of opposite logical lanes, native `LaneOverlap` injection, graph restriction, entry enforcement, car braking/speed integration, native reservation adapter or lane-change actuator. All game traffic activation is unavailable.
- The installed native overlap generator skips ordinary edge-lane pairs. Automatic full-length reciprocal overlaps must not be assumed. Static inspection does not establish whether manually supplied opposing overlaps brake safely; that still requires the isolated native experiment.
- Reverse-curve diagnostics compare all four cubic control points within 5 cm and reject ambiguous endpoints. They deliberately miss differently parameterized equivalent curves, partial overlaps and closed loops. Candidate detection is geometry evidence only, never permission to activate traffic.
- Configuration validation accepts explicit physical strip classifications. A read-only Road Builder adapter now evaluates only layouts made entirely from the verified `Car Drive Section 3` and virtual-lane graph-test sections; it withholds a result for unknown sections or lane groups. It does not run before generation, classify placed-network access, or enable traffic. Runtime evidence is caller-supplied; it is not inferred from member presence.
- The core runtime validator now requires nine named evidence values, and unknown or failed checks withhold activation. No game adapter supplies these checks yet. A caller must tie each positive result to the current placed corridor and network revision; the core cannot independently verify that provenance.
- The one-shot Road Builder cross-section diagnostic now prints a separate `runtimeStatus` using all-unknown safety evidence. It cannot activate a road. Installed `PathUtils.GetCarDriveSpecification` assigns a cost to `CarLaneFlags.Forbidden` and still creates a forward path edge, so that flag alone is not a hard turn-only route restriction.
- Core calculations support arbitrary compatible lane counts, asymmetry and both handedness conventions, but this has only been tested in the standalone model.
- Full route swept intervals are conservative, including for same-direction vehicles. Space is not reused merely because an ETA passed. Fairness/priority ageing and native lane-object occupancy reconstruction are not implemented.
- Overdue occupied vehicles retain safety holds and close admissions. Automatic stuck-vehicle recovery needs real occupancy, a verified exit and native navigation integration. Releasing a hold by timeout would be unsafe.
- Gap advice assumes complete local observations and constant-speed neighbours. It considers three acceleration choices; it is not an exhaustive search and may yield despite a feasible gap. No result authorizes a lane change. Hysteresis currently affects preferences among candidate gaps and is tested for unchanged-observation stability, not fluctuating game traffic.
- Intersection transition intervals are calculated; actual lane geometry, turn connection generation and signals are not changed. Short segments get a closed fallback.
- Tram support is not runtime-tested. Crossable tracks produce a configuration WARNING; impassable tracks are INVALID. Native turning-car/tram conflict processing is unknown.
- One local save/load in the same process preserved the isolated closed road's Road Builder cross-section and inspectable lane count. Game restart, updates, mod disposal and removal have not been tested with research roads. Road Builder documents rebuilding roads without missing custom sections. This can change a road layout; do not assume uninstall safety or general save compatibility. A startup warning said the custom section could not be found before later picker/edit success; its lifecycle implications remain open.
- No automated compatibility enforcement for other mods exists. The API audit logs assemblies loaded at that instant; it can miss later-loaded assemblies. Source review identified overlapping systems but no combined playset was tested.
- The current managed controller allocates during batches and reconciliation, is not thread-safe or Burst-ready, and has no measured large-city budget. Diagnostic captures may synchronize jobs and stall; they run only when requested and disclose truncated counts.
- Only actual research controls appear in game options. Intended/exit/urgent distances, speed, safety clearance and transition length are configurable constructor parameters in the tested core; player-facing traffic settings and debug visualization are intentionally not exposed until they have a working game adapter.
- No Paradox dependency metadata/package or release is provided. Road Builder is the intended required mod; the others are optional research/compatibility targets.
